The breakdown

A process that worked at $400M revenue broke at $1.8B.

XYZ Corp didn't have a technology problem so much as a seams problem — every acquisition added another spreadsheet between the ERP and the bank.

The problem

Cash was stuck in the gaps

Finance closed the quarter on adrenaline. Controllers reconciled 34 entities by hand, and no one could answer "how much are we actually owed?" without a two-day fire drill.

  • 21-day invoice cycleOrders touched 6 systems and 4 manual handoffs before an invoice ever left the building.
  • 32% of payments unmatchedLegacy rules couldn't read short-pays, consolidated remittances, or 11 currencies.
  • 14-day financial closeTwo weeks of nights and weekends, every quarter, for a team of 22.
  • $6.8M in aged ARDisputes surfaced 45 days late — after the relationship had already soured.
  • A rip-and-replace quoteThe ERP upgrade path was priced at $9M over 30 months. The board said no.
The solution

We built on top, not instead of

Northwind Flow sat between SAP and the bank as a system of action: ingesting every order, invoice, and remittance, then resolving the exceptions humans used to chase.

  • Non-invasive ERP syncRead/write connectors to SAP ECC and Salesforce. Zero schema changes, zero downtime.
  • Adaptive cash matchingLearns each customer's payment behavior — short-pays, netting, batch remittance — to 99.4% accuracy.
  • Exception-first workspaceAnalysts see only the 0.6% that needs judgment, with the full audit trail attached.
  • Continuous closeSub-ledgers reconcile nightly, so day one of close looks like day nine used to.
  • One cash position34 entities, 11 currencies, refreshed every 15 minutes for the CFO and treasury.

How it happened

Eleven weeks, no big bang.

Every phase shipped value on its own. If the pilot hadn't worked, XYZ Corp could have walked away in week three.

Weeks 1–2Pilot

Three entities, read-only

Connected SAP ECC and two bank feeds in a sandbox. Replayed 90 days of historical remittance to prove match accuracy against known outcomes before touching a live ledger.

96.1% match on replayed history
Weeks 3–5Go live

First production cash application

Flipped the pilot entities to write-back. Analysts reviewed every automated decision for two weeks, then approved a confidence threshold to run unattended.

Cycle time halved in month one
Weeks 6–9Scale

34 entities, 11 currencies

Rolled out region by region with the same template. Treasury replaced its Monday cash-position spreadsheet with a live dashboard in week seven.

Zero ERP schema changes
Weeks 10–11Close

Continuous reconciliation on

Nightly sub-ledger reconciliation and automated dispute routing turned quarter-end from an event into a checkpoint. The first close under the new process took four days.

Clean audit, no material findings
