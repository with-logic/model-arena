The story

We stopped asking
what else it could do.

Every smartwatch generation adds features and subtracts battery. Meridian One inverts the trade. One custom low‑power silicon platform, one honest promise: it measures what matters, and it does not die on you.

14 days

Battery, not a rounding error

The M1 co‑processor runs sensing, haptics and the always‑on display at 1.1 mW. Two weeks typical use. Six with GPS on continuously.

3,000 nits

Legible in a July noon

A 1.9″ LTPO4 micro‑OLED that ramps 1–120 Hz and dims to a single nit for night. Sapphire crystal, anti‑reflective on both faces.

6 signals

Sensing you can act on

Lead‑I ECG, continuous PPG, skin temperature, SpO₂, bioimpedance and a 32 g accelerometer — fused into one recovery score, not twelve dashboards.
