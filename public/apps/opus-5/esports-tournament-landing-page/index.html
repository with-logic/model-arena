Venue & Travel

Neo Kyoto Dome

42,000 seats. Six minutes from Kyoto Station on the Karasuma Line. Doors open two hours before the first serve each day.

Neo Kyoto Dome

1-Chome-7 Higashishiokoji, Shimogyo Ward, Kyoto 600-8216, Japan

Directions Transit & parking Accessibility desk

Step-free access

Level entry at Gates A & D, 340 wheelchair spaces with companion seating, and lifts to every concourse tier.

Sensory & quiet rooms

Two low-stimulus rooms on the west concourse, free ear defenders at Guest Services, and a strobe-free viewing zone in Block 118.

Safety & safeguarding

Bag checks at all gates, medical posts on each tier, and a 24/7 anonymous reporting line — text SAFE to 5150 from inside the venue.

Live captions

Open captions on all arena screens plus a captioned companion stream in EN/JP/KO — scan the QR at any seat block.

Travel notes

Kansai International (KIX) is 75 min by Haruka Express. A tournament shuttle runs every 20 min from Kyoto Station Hachijo Exit, 09:00–01:00 on match days. Show your ticket QR for free travel on the Karasuma and Tozai lines all weekend.
