Six months, one variable at a time

About twenty variables decide how espresso tastes. The program takes them in an order that builds: control the shot first, then make the coffee you’re pulling. Two 90-minute sessions in your kitchen each month, with shot reviews in between.

Why six months? Most members pull their first great shot in month two. Roasting takes longer to click, and it’s what makes the shot yours. Month to month gets you the espresso kit; the roaster and the curriculum come with the full program.

Questions people ask before joining

Something else on your mind? Text us at (415) 555-0137 and a coach will answer, usually within the hour.

What does $200 a month cover?

Everything on the kit list, delivered and installed. Two coaching sessions in your kitchen each month, shot and roast reviews between visits, 2 lb of green coffee a month, Saturday cuppings at the Lab, and all maintenance. The only thing you buy is milk, if you drink it.

Why six months instead of month to month?

Because roasting takes that long to click. The first two months are about controlling the shot. The roaster arrives in month three, and months four to six are where your own coffee gets good.

Month to month exists for people who only want the espresso kit. It costs more because delivery and setup are the same work either way.

What if I move, or need to stop early?

In the first 14 days, tell us and we’ll refund everything and collect the kit. After that, you can end the program at any time for a $150 pickup fee. No remaining months are charged.

What happens after month six?

You choose. Return the kit and we pick it up. Keep going on a maintenance plan at $160 a month with one session a month. Or buy the kit at its refurbished price, minus 40% of the fees you’ve paid.

What if something breaks?

Normal wear is on us: gaskets, burrs, descaling, a failed pump. Your coach fixes it or swaps the machine within 48 hours. Accidental damage has a $150 deductible.

Can I roast in an apartment?

Usually, yes. Light and medium roasts make modest smoke, and most members roast under a range hood on high. We check your ventilation at install and include a vent kit for a nearby window. If your space won’t work, you can roast on our machines at the Lab on Saturdays instead.

How much time does it take?

Plan on two to three hours a week, most of it making the coffee you’d drink anyway. Coaching visits run 90 minutes, and we schedule evenings and Saturdays.

I already own a good machine. Can I keep using it?

Yes. Tell us when you reserve and we’ll install only what you’re missing. The price stays the same and you get a third session each month. If your machine doesn’t record pressure and flow, shot reviews work from short videos instead.
