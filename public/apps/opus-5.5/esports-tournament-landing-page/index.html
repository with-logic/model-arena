      Accessibility

      • Step-free entry at Gates A and C, with lifts to every level.
      • Wheelchair spaces in every price tier, each with a companion seat. Choose accessible seating at checkout.
      • Live captions in Korean and English on the main screen for every match and ceremony.
      • Hearing loop in sections 104 to 108, plus loaner headsets at the info desk.
      • Quiet room on level 2 from doors to close. Free ear plugs at every info desk.
      • Low-strobe seating in sections 120 to 122. Strobe effects are announced on screen 60 seconds ahead.
      • Assistance dogs are welcome everywhere in the venue.

      Safety

      • Clear bags only, up to 30 × 30 × 15 cm. Small clutches are fine.
      • Not allowed: glass, laser pointers, air horns, drones and flags on poles.
      • Medical points at Gates A and D and at the floor entrance.
      • Report harassment to any steward in a magenta vest, or text the safety line printed on your ticket. We reply within five minutes.
      • Lost children point at the Gate A info desk. Add your phone number to a child wristband at entry.
      • Re-entry is allowed with your wristband until 18:00.

      Questions before you go

      Can I leave and come back in?

      Yes. Keep your wristband on and you can re-enter until 18:00 each day. After 18:00, entry is one way.

      Are tickets refundable?

      Full refunds until 1 February 2027. After that, you can resell at face value through the official exchange, which opens on 14 February.

      Is there an age limit?

      Everyone is welcome. Under-14s need an adult with them, and under-3s sit on a lap for free.

      Can I bring food and drink?

      Sealed water bottles up to 500 ml are fine. Food stalls on level 1 cover vegetarian, halal and gluten-free options.

      How were the teams seeded?

      By points from the 2026 regional circuits. The top team from each region was placed in a different quarter of the bracket, so they can’t meet before the semifinals.

      Do I need to pay to watch online?

      No. Every match streams free on Twitch, YouTube, SOOP and CHZZK, and full replays go up on YouTube within an hour.

      When do players sign autographs?

      Signing sessions run on the fan stage at 10:00 each day. Floor VIP ticket holders get a reserved slot with the team of their choice.

      Code of conduct

      This applies to everyone at Arclight: fans, players, staff, press and partners, in the arena and in our chats.

      1. Treat everyone with respect.Players, staff, other fans and people in chat.
      2. No harassment, hate speech or discrimination.This covers what you say, wear, write on signs and post online.
      3. Leave the player area alone.Don’t touch booths or equipment, and keep clear of the player walkway.
      4. Keep signs smaller than A2.Stewards may ask you to lower a sign that blocks someone’s view.
      5. Don’t restream the broadcast.Short clips for social media are fine. Full matches are not.
      6. Follow steward instructions.Anyone who breaks these rules can be removed without a refund.

      To report something, find a steward in a magenta vest or text the safety line printed on your ticket. Reports are confidential.
