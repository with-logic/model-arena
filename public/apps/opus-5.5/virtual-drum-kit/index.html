Playing the kit

Each key plays the pad in the same spot on the grid. Space also plays the kick. Clicking near the middle of a pad hits harder than clicking near its edge.

Recording a take

  • With Click off, Rec arms the recorder and the take starts on your first hit. Press Stop when you reach the end of your loop.
  • With Click on, you get a one-bar count-in and the take is trimmed to whole bars, so it loops cleanly.
  • Overdub keeps the take looping while you add hits on top. Undo removes the last pass.
  • Your take and settings are saved in this browser.
