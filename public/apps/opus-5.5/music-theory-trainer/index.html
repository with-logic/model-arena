What you played, written out

Seven letters on repeat

White keys run C D E F G A B, then start over at the next C. Find C just left of each pair of black keys.

Black keys have two names

The black key after C is C♯ (C sharp). It is also D♭ (D flat). Same sound, two spellings.

Octaves are numbered

Middle C is C4. The next C up is C5. Notes with the same letter sound alike, just higher or lower.

Half steps and whole steps

Neighbouring keys are a half step apart. Skip one key for a whole step. E–F and B–C have no black key between them.

Intervals

An interval is the distance between two notes. Count it in semitones: every key, black or white, is one step. Pick a starting note on the circle.
