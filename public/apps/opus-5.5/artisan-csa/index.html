Eat what the field gives, the week it gives it.

Artisan CSA is a share in one eighteen-acre farm in New York’s Rondout Valley. Every week, 340 households split what we picked that morning: greens, roots, fruit, and the odd surprise, left at the door or at a pickup near you.

What comes in the box

The box follows the field, so it changes every few weeks. Hover over a season, or use “What’s usually inside”, to see the produce we pack most years. The small tags flag common allergens and nightshades.

    About the tags Everything is packed in one barn, so traces can travel. If you have a serious allergy, tell us when you sign up and we’ll leave flagged items out of your box.

    Build your share

    Choose a box size, how often it comes, and anything extra from our neighbors. The tag keeps the running total. Billed monthly; pause or cancel with two days’ notice.

    Box size

    Both sizes get the same variety. The full share gets more of each thing.

    How often

    Every-other-week boxes cost $3 more each: the truck drives the same loop for fewer stops.

          Where the truck goes

          The truck drives a 25-mile loop around the farm four afternoons a week. Inside the loop your box comes to the door. Anywhere, you can collect it from one of seven pickup spots.

          Pickup spots

          Distances from the farm

            Doorstep delivery is free inside the loop. Boxes arrive between 2 and 7 p.m. on your town’s day, in a waxed crate we collect the next week.
