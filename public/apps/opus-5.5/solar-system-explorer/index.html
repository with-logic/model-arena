    Planet positions come from NASA JPL’s Keplerian orbital elements, so the arrangement you see matches the real sky for the date shown. Moon phases, Earth’s day and night and the tilt of each world’s seasons follow the same clock. Distances are compressed and sizes enlarged so everything fits on one screen.
