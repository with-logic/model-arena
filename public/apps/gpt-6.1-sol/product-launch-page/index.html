A few good questions.

The little things you might be wondering before you find your rhythm.

When will Ora One arrive?

Our first edition is planned for spring 2027. This concept preview lets you explore the product and save a reservation locally. It doesn't place a real order or sign you up for emails.

Will it work with my phone?

Ora One is designed to pair with iPhones running iOS 16 or later and Android phones running Android 10 or later through the Ora companion app. A connected phone is needed for messages and notifications.

Can I take it for a swim?

With a 5 ATM water-resistance design, Ora One is made for everyday splashes and shallow-water swimming. It isn't designed for diving, high-pressure water, hot showers, or saunas.

Do I need a subscription?

No subscription is planned for Ora One's everyday features, including activity tracking, sleep insights, and the companion app. Health insights support general wellness and aren't intended for medical diagnosis.
