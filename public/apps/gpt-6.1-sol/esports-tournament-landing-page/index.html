GOOD TO KNOW

Who can compete?

Teams of five players aged 16 and over can register. Players under 18 need guardian consent. All regions are welcome; the tournament is played on the NEXUS tactical arena demo.

What's included with my ticket?

The $89 arena pass covers all three days, the fan zone, team meetups, and a NEXUS lanyard. A $39 day pass covers one selected day. These are demo reservations; no payment is taken.

Can I watch online for free?

Yes. The broadcast is designed to be free to watch. Try our animated broadcast preview above. This concept site does not carry an actual live stream.

Is the venue accessible?

The event plan includes step-free routes, accessible seating, companion spaces, and a quiet room. Contact the team below with your access needs before reserving.
