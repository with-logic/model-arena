The overview

Growing fast shouldn’t mean
getting tangled.

For XYZ Corp, growth was a good problem to have. But as their team expanded, the way they worked started getting in the way. More tools. More handoffs. Less time to move great ideas forward.

They needed a simpler way to bring everyone together. With Align, they found one shared workspace—and a whole new rhythm.

The challenge

Great people.
Disconnected work.

Eight tools, endless status updates, and no single source of truth. Keeping work moving had become a job of its own.

  • Projects scattered across tools
  • Manual handoffs slowing teams down
  • Decisions made without the full picture
The solution

One workspace.
A shared way forward.

Align connected their projects, automated the routine, and gave every team a clear view of what comes next.

  • All their work, together in one place
  • Automations that keep things moving
  • Real-time visibility for better decisions

A fresh start, without starting over.

XYZ Corp rolled out Align one team at a time, connecting the tools they already loved and building workflows around the way they actually work.
