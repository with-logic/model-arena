A few things you
might be wondering.

CSA stands for Community Supported Agriculture. You buy a regular share of a farm’s harvest, giving farmers a dependable home for their crops. In return, you get fresh food, a connection to the land, and a delicious reason to cook.

Each share follows the season, so the harvest chooses your menu. Expect 6–8 varieties, plus simple recipes and storage tips. If something is new to your kitchen, we’ll help you find a delicious use for it.

Life happens. Let the farm know at least 3 days before your delivery to skip or pause a share. You can also gift your box to a friend. There’s no long-term commitment, and you can cancel before your next harvest.

The produce share is entirely plant-based and contains no major allergens by recipe. Eggs contain egg; sourdough contains wheat and gluten. Add-ons are packed separately, but we can’t guarantee an allergen-free packing space. Contact us to discuss your needs.

Local delivery is included in your share price. Check your ZIP above to see our sample Portland delivery area. Prefer to say hello? Choose a neighborhood pickup and collect your share during its listed hours. Bring your own bag or return last week’s box.
