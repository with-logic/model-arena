The Pomodoro technique makes big things feel a little smaller.

  1. Pick one thing.

    Give your session a purpose. Choose a task small enough to make a start.

  2. Give it your focus.

    Work for 25 minutes, or choose your own duration. Keep distractions for later.

  3. Take a real break.

    Stretch, look outside, get some water. After four focus sessions, take a longer breather.
