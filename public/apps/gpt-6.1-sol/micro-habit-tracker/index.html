A little guide to micro.

A simple space to build habits at your own pace.

  • Show up, check in.Click a box to complete a habit. Click again to undo. The weekly view lets you fill in earlier days.
  • Find your rhythm.Consecutive completed days build your streak. Today stays open until the day ends. A missed day resets it.
  • Make room for rest.Use a habit’s ··· menu to skip a day. Skips protect your streak and are excluded from the weekly completion rate.
  • Your habits, your device.Your progress saves in this browser. Export a JSON backup to keep it safe or move it to another device. The example habits are yours to edit or delete.
