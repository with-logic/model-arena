Write it the way you think.

Enter one equation with one variable. Equate solves linear and quadratic equations, including parentheses, fractions, decimals, and complex roots. An expression without an equals sign is treated as equal to zero.

Powersx^2 or x²
Multiplication2x or 2*x
Fractionsx/2 + 1 = 4
Parentheses3(x − 2) = 12

Every step has a reason.

We simplify both sides first. Linear equations are solved by isolating the variable. For quadratics, we factor when the roots are rational; otherwise, we use the quadratic formula. The graph plots the left side minus the right side, so its x-intercepts are your real solutions.

Equations with variables in denominators, multiple variables, trigonometry, or degrees above two aren’t supported yet.
