    Venue & travel

    Halcyon Arena, 1 Neon Way, Los Angeles, CA. Doors open 90 minutes before the first match each day.

    Getting there

    Driving Transit Walking Bike

    Take the Blue Line to the Circuit Ave stop, then walk four minutes north. Pre-book Garage C for parking; event shuttles run from Union Station every 15 minutes.

    Accessibility

    • Step-free entrances at Circuit Ave and Gate 3, with lifts to every level.
    • Wheelchair spaces and companion seats. Request them through the contact form, topic “Accessibility request”.
    • ASL interpreters on the Main Stage each day; captions on the stream.
    • Sensory-friendly quiet room with dimmed lights, open all three days.
    • Stage lighting uses flashing effects. A reduced-flash viewing zone is in Zone C.

    Safety

    • Clear-bag policy. Bags larger than A4 are not admitted.
    • Medical station on Level 1, staffed for the full event day.
    • Lost property and emergency desk at Gate 1.
    • Free water refill stations throughout the concourse.
    • Code of conduct enforced on site. Report incidents to any staff member.

    Questions

    FAQ

    Can I bring my own peripherals?

    Yes. Keyboards, mice, headsets and controllers are all welcome. Rig inspection runs from 8:00 to 10:00 each morning in the player lounge.

    What is the minimum age to attend?

    Ages 13 and up are welcome. Attendees under 18 need a signed guardian waiver, which is available in the ticket confirmation email.

    How do teams register?

    Use the Register Team button. Rosters lock 30 days before opening day, and every player needs a verified account for anti-cheat checks.

    Are tickets refundable?

    Yes, until February 12, 2027, minus a 10% processing fee. After that date, tickets can be transferred but not refunded.

    Where can I watch the matches?

    Every main-stage match streams live with English and Spanish commentary. Replays are posted within 24 hours, with captions.
