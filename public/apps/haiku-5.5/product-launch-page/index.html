Meridian unveils Watch, a health companion designed to disappear until you need it.

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 13, 2026 — Meridian today announced Meridian Watch, a precision health and fitness wearable that pairs a 1.9-inch always-on display with clinical-grade sensors and up to seven days of battery life. Pre-orders open today at $299, with first shipments beginning in December 2026.

Meridian Watch was designed around a simple idea: the best health data is the data you never have to think about. Continuous heart-rate and blood-oxygen monitoring run quietly in the background, while nightly sleep staging and recovery scores appear only when they are useful.

The watch features dual-band GPS for accurate routes in dense cities and remote trails alike, 5 ATM water resistance for swimming, and a sapphire crystal over a 316L stainless steel case. A magnetic dock charges from zero to 80 percent in 30 minutes.
