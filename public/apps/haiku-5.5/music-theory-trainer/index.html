🎯 Pocket Theory

Half step = the next key, up or down. Whole step = two keys.

Major scale: whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole, half.

A major triad is a major 3rd (4 semitones) stacked under a minor 3rd (3 semitones). Minor flips them.

Middle C is C4. Each octave holds 12 semitones: 7 white keys and 5 black keys.
