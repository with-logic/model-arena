The six-month residency

A curriculum that ends with your signature espresso.

Six months is long enough to build real intuition and short enough to stay motivated. Each month has a clear outcome, a set of practice goals, and a coach-reviewed graduation pull.

Calibrate

Learn the physics of extraction and set up your bench correctly. Fix the basics before you chase flavor.

  • Water chemistry & grinder setup
  • Dose, yield, and ratio discipline
  • First 50 logged shots

Dial in

Build a repeatable recipe for three coffees. Learn to change one variable at a time and predict the result.

  • Taste-driven grind adjustments
  • Pre-infusion & temperature profiling
  • Sensory vocabulary and cupping basics

Roast

Move upstream. Roast your own batches, read the profile curves, and understand how roast affects the cup.

  • First batches on the drum roaster
  • Reading development time & RoR
  • Blending roasts for espresso

Origin

Compare coffees side by side. Learn how process, altitude, and variety change the way a shot tastes.

  • Blind comparison across 4 origins
  • Natural vs. washed processing
  • Writing your own tasting notes

Milk & texture

Make the milk drinks as well as the espresso. Steam, microfoam, and latte art fundamentals with a coach.

  • Milk texturing technique
  • Latte art basics
  • Milk-based recipe calibration

Signature

Design, roast, and present your own espresso. Graduate with a recipe card, a roast profile, and a coach-reviewed tasting.

  • Build your signature blend
  • Graduation tasting with your coach
  • Recipe card you keep forever

Most members hit their signature recipe by week 18.
