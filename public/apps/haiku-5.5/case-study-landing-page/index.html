The challenge

Growth was outpacing the tools meant to support it.

XYZ Corp was adding regions and customers quickly, but its data lived in disconnected systems. Leaders were making high-stakes calls on numbers that were already days old.

Manual reporting ate the week

Analysts copied data from five systems into spreadsheets every Monday. The output was often out of date before it reached a meeting.

16 hrsper week, per analyst, spent on reconciliation

Conflicting numbers in every room

Each department defined revenue, churn, and capacity differently. Executive reviews opened with arguments about the data instead of decisions.

3 versionsof the same quarterly revenue figure in circulation

Problems surfaced too late

Churn signals and fulfillment delays were visible only after customers had already started to leave. Teams reacted instead of acting.

30+ daysaverage lag before churn risk reached account teams

Before the platform

  • Five disconnected data sources with no shared definitions
  • Weekly reports built by hand, then rebuilt for each region
  • Decisions delayed while teams reconciled numbers

What leadership needed

  • One governed view of revenue, operations, and customers
  • Live dashboards that analysts could trust without checking
  • Early warning on churn and delivery risk
The solution

One platform, connected to everything, rolled out in six weeks.

XYZ Corp's team worked alongside our solutions engineers to connect their data, define shared metrics, and move reporting out of spreadsheets, one team at a time.

01

Connect every source

Native connectors pulled data from the ERP, CRM, warehouse management, and billing systems into one governed model.

  • No-code pipelines with automatic schema updates
  • Row-level permissions matched to existing roles
02

Agree on one set of definitions

Finance, operations, and sales signed off on a shared metrics layer, so every dashboard used the same math.

  • Certified metrics with owners and change history
  • Version-controlled definitions across regions
03

Act on alerts, not hindsight

Automated alerts flagged churn risk and delivery delays, routing them directly to the account and operations teams that own them.

  • Threshold and anomaly alerts in Slack and email
  • Playbooks triggered from high-risk accounts
