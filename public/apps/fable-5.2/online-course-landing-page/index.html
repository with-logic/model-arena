Before you enroll

Still deciding? These are the things learners ask us most. If yours isn't here, the syllabus usually answers it.

Do I need any experience to start?

Courses marked Beginner assume none — just curiosity and a laptop. Intermediate courses list what you should already know at the top of the syllabus, so you can check before enrolling.

How much time will I need each week?

Plan for five to eight hours. Lessons are short, labs are where the time goes. Self-paced learners can spread a course out for as long as they like; cohorts follow a weekly rhythm.

What's the difference between self-paced and live cohort?

The lessons and projects are identical. Cohorts add a fixed schedule, weekly live sessions with your instructor, a personal mentor and faster project reviews. Self-paced is cheaper and starts the moment you enroll.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Complete every module project and you'll receive a verifiable certificate — though we think the projects in your portfolio will speak louder.

What if it isn't right for me?

Tell us within 14 days of starting and we'll refund you in full, no form to fill in and no awkward call.
