F/01POST /v1/deploys

Deploy

Point at a repo and a ref. SLAB builds it, boots it on bare metal in every region you name, and shifts traffic when it is healthy.

F/02PUT /v1/kv/:ns/:key

Store

A key-value store replicated to every region. Writes are durable before they return. Reads come from the closest rack.

F/03GET /v1/deploys/:id/logs

Stream

Every log line from every region as one ordered stream. Filter on the server, tail it from your terminal or your code.
