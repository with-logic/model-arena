    Venue & travel

    Voltwerk Arena

    A converted power station on the Spree, ten minutes from Berlin Ostbahnhof. Doors open 90 minutes before the first match each day.

    Accessibility

    • Step-free access from Gate B, with lifts to every level and 86 wheelchair bays with companion seating.
    • Hearing loops at all info desks; live captions on the in-arena screens and the stream.
    • Quiet room on Level 1 with dimmed lighting and ear defenders to borrow, free of charge.
    • Assistance animals welcome. Free companion tickets on request: access@neonclash.gg.
    • Strobe warning: team walk-outs use flashing lights. Low-sensory seating is in Block 114.

    Safety

    • Bag policy: one bag up to A4 size per person. Lockers are available at Gate A for €4.
    • Security screening at every entrance. Allow an extra 20 minutes before headline matches.
    • First aid points on each level, staffed all day. Any steward can call a medic for you.
    • Cashless venue. Free water refill stations beside every food court.
    • Need help? Text SAFE to 60 330 or find a steward in a lime vest. Reports are confidential.

    FAQs

    When do doors open?

    Doors open 90 minutes before the first match of each day: 10:30 CET Thursday and Friday, 13:30 on Saturday and 12:30 on Sunday. Re-entry is allowed with a wristband.

    Can I get a refund or resell my ticket?

    Tickets are refundable until 11 February 2027. After that you can transfer or resell at face value through your ticket account, right up to the day.

    Is there an age limit?

    All ages are welcome. Under-14s need to come with an adult, and under-16s need a signed consent form for the floor standing area.

    How does my team register?

    Open qualifiers run in four regions from 9 January. Hit “Register Team”, add five players and a substitute, and we'll email your seeding and match day details.

    Where can I watch if I can't make it?

    Every match streams free in 1080p60 with commentary in 14 languages. VODs go up within an hour of each series ending.

    Can I bring a camera or signs?

    Phones and compact cameras are fine. Detachable lenses, tripods and signs larger than A2 aren't allowed in the bowl.
