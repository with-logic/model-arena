The plateau

You've watched every video. The shot is still sour.

Great espresso isn't a purchase. It's a feedback loop — and nobody sells you one of those.

  • 01

    The gear is a $9,000 guess.

    Flat or conical burrs? Lever, E61 or pressure-profiling? Forums will happily spend your money for you. You only find out what you actually love after you've lived with it.

  • 02

    You're changing five variables at once.

    Dose, grind, temperature, ratio, pre-infusion — plus a bag that ages every day. Without someone tasting alongside you, it's uncontrolled experimentation with a caffeine budget.

  • 03

    The real lever is upstream.

    The single biggest input to the cup is the roast — and it's the one variable you've never been able to touch. Until it's sitting on your counter.

Can I really roast coffee in an apartment?

Yes. The roaster is electric and runs on a standard 120 V / 15 A outlet. At the 350 g batch size we teach, smoke is roughly what you'd get searing a steak. We install a window vent kit and filter on day one, and about a third of our members roast in condos and apartments in SF.

How much space and time does this take?

About 40 inches of counter for the espresso bench; the roaster lives on a rolling cart we provide. Time-wise, plan on one roast session a week (about 45 minutes), your daily shots, and two coaching sessions a month. Most members say it replaced an hour of scrolling, not an hour of family time.

I already own a decent machine or grinder. Is this still for me?

Often, yes — about 40% of residents arrive with gear. Tell us on your fit call. We can build your bench around what you own and bring only what you're missing; coaching, curriculum and green coffee stay the same.

What happens when the six months are up?

Three options, your call: keep going at $200 a month with no further commitment; buy any or all of the equipment with 35% of everything you've paid ($420) credited toward the price; or we pick it all up, free. No auto-renewal into a new term.

What if I break something?

Normal wear, scale build-up, a dropped portafilter, a cracked carafe — all covered, with a swap inside 48 hours. You're only responsible for loss or deliberate damage. We service every machine between residents and descale on a schedule matched to your water.

I travel for work. Can I pause?

You get one free 30-day pause during the residency; your six months simply extend by a month. Shorter trips don't need one — your coach will re-sequence sessions around your calendar.

What exactly is the six-month commitment?

Six monthly payments of $200, starting on install day. The first 30 days are covered by the Dialed-In Guarantee — full refund, we collect the gear. After that, you can leave early by paying half of the remaining months. In practice, 91% of residents finish.

Where do you serve?

San Francisco, the Peninsula, South Bay, the East Bay through Walnut Creek and Fremont, and Marin up to Novato. Enter your ZIP when you reserve and we'll confirm instantly. Outside that? Join the waitlist — Santa Cruz and Sonoma are next.
