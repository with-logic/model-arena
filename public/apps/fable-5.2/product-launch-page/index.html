Halden unveils Meridian One, a titanium smartwatch built to be worn for days and noticed only when it matters

Nine-day battery life, a medical-grade sensor array and a new “Quiet Hours” intelligence engine arrive in a 38-gram case. Pre-orders open today from $349.

COPENHAGEN, 13 November 2026 — Halden, the Danish design and technology studio, today announced Meridian One, its first smartwatch. Machined from a single billet of Grade 5 titanium and finished under a dome of sapphire crystal, Meridian One pairs the proportions of a classic field watch with the most efficient platform Halden has ever built.

At its core is the new H1 chip, a dual-core processor fabricated on a 4 nm process that sips power in everyday use and hands off to a dedicated low-energy co-processor whenever the display dims. The result is up to nine days of typical use on the 45 mm model, and a full day of charge from a ten-minute top-up.

“We didn’t set out to build a smaller phone for your wrist. We set out to build a watch you forget to charge, and never need to silence.”

Ingrid Solvang, Co-founder & Chief Executive, Halden

A redesigned six-channel optical array, a single-lead ECG and a skin-temperature sensor work together to track heart rhythm, blood oxygen, sleep stages and recovery through the night. Every reading is processed on the watch itself; health data is end-to-end encrypted and is never sold or used for advertising.

Meridian One also introduces Quiet Hours, an on-device model that learns which notifications you act on and holds the rest for a single, calm summary. In a twelve-week pilot, wearers received 61% fewer interruptions without missing a message they cared about.

Meridian One is available to pre-order today in 41 mm and 45 mm sizes and four finishes, priced from $349. Deliveries begin 2 February 2027 in 22 countries.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    When will I be charged?

    Not until your watch leaves our warehouse. We’ll email you five days before dispatch so you can confirm, change or cancel your reservation.

    Does it work with my phone?

    Yes. Meridian One pairs with iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Android (version 12 or later) through the Halden app. Every health feature works on both.

    Can I change my configuration later?

    You can change finish, size, band or engraving from the link in your confirmation email until your order enters production — typically three weeks before dispatch.

    What’s in the box?

    Meridian One, your chosen band in S/M and M/L lengths, a magnetic USB-C fast-charge puck, and a quick-start card. Packaging is fully recyclable and plastic-free.

    Is my health data private?

    Health readings are processed on the watch and end-to-end encrypted when synced. Halden cannot read them, and will never sell them or use them for advertising.
