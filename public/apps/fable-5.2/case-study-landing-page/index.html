Challenge & solution

Three problems stood between a signed contract and a live customer.

By 2024 XYZ Corp was closing 40 new accounts a quarter, and every one of them queued behind the same bottlenecks. Here is what was broken, what replaced it, and what changed.

Problem 01

Onboarding lived in six disconnected tools.

Contracts sat in the CRM, requirements in spreadsheets, tasks in email threads. Every handoff meant re-keying details, and customers were asked the same questions three times.

With Meridian

One shared workspace, from signature to go-live.

Meridian syncs with XYZ Corp’s CRM and billing system, so each new account opens with a pre-filled plan that the customer and the internal team work from together.

Handoffs per account 9 → 2
Problem 02

Customers waited two weeks for their data.

Every shipper’s carrier rates and lane history had to be mapped by hand by an engineer. Data setup alone took 14 days and pulled the product team off the roadmap.

With Meridian

Self-serve imports that map themselves.

Customers upload their files and Meridian matches the fields, flags errors and validates the result in minutes. Engineers only step in for genuine edge cases.

Data setup 14 → 3 days
Problem 03

Nobody knew which accounts were stuck.

Leadership found out about stalled launches when customers escalated. Nearly one in five new accounts cancelled before their first renewal.

With Meridian

Live health scores and stall alerts.

Every account carries a health score. If a launch goes quiet for 48 hours, the owner gets an alert and a suggested next step, well before the customer has to ask.

First-year churn 19% → 6%

How it rolled out

Live for every new customer in twelve weeks.

No big-bang migration. XYZ Corp ran Meridian alongside the old process, proved it on a small pilot, then switched over.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Map the process

    Meridian’s team shadowed five live onboardings and documented all 63 steps. Twenty-two were cut outright.

  2. Weeks 3–6

    Connect the stack

    CRM, billing and the data warehouse were connected, and existing playbooks became reusable templates.

  3. Weeks 7–10

    Pilot with 12 accounts

    The pilot group went live in a median of 16 days. Feedback shaped the import flow and alert rules.

  4. Week 12

    Full rollout

    Every new account now starts in Meridian. The legacy spreadsheets were archived the same day.
