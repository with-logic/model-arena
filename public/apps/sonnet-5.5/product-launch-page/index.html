Press release

Meridian unveils the watch that finally gets out of your way.

SAN FRANCISCO · NOVEMBER 13, 2026

Meridian today announced Meridian One, its first smartwatch, built around a simple idea: a watch should be glanceable, durable, and charged when you need it. Machined from a single block of grade-5 titanium, it weighs just 42 grams and pairs a sapphire-covered LTPO display with a battery that lasts two weeks.

Meridian One introduces the M-Sense array, a four-in-one sensor capable of continuous heart rhythm, blood oxygen, skin temperature, and ECG readings, all processed on-device so health data never leaves your wrist unless you choose.

“We removed everything that demanded your attention, and kept everything that earns it.”
— Lena Hartmann, Founder & CEO, Meridian

Meridian One is available to pre-order today starting at $449, with deliveries beginning December 12, 2026.
