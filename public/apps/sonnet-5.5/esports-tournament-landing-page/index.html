    07 // FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Do I need a ticket to attend all three days?

    Day passes cover a single day. The Finals Weekend pass covers Saturday and Sunday. VIP covers all three days with lounge access.

    How do teams qualify and register?

    16 teams qualify through regional circuits. Open registration is for the Challengers Showdown on Dec 10 — top two earn a wildcard seat.

    Is the event streamed in my language?

    Yes — English, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish, Japanese and German broadcasts, plus ASL and captioned feeds.

    What is the refund policy?

    Tickets are refundable up to 14 days before the event, and transferable up to 24 hours prior via your account.

    Can I bring a camera or streaming gear?

    Phones and compact cameras are fine. Tripods, lenses over 70mm and streaming rigs require a media pass.

    Code of Conduct

    Everyone at Neon Major — players, fans, staff and press — agrees to:

    1. Respect. No harassment, hate speech, discrimination or threats, online or in person.
    2. Fair play. No cheating, match-fixing, or exploiting unintended bugs.
    3. Consent. Ask before photos or touching; no means no.
    4. Safety first. Follow staff direction and keep exits clear.
    5. Report. Text 55-NEON or email conduct@neonmajor.gg — reports are confidential and answered within 24 hours.

    Violations may result in removal without refund and bans from future events.
