The challenge

As XYZ Corp grew, work became scattered across documents, messages, and disconnected tools. Teams spent too much time tracking down updates and too little time moving projects forward. They needed a clearer way to work together.

02

The solution

With Forma, XYZ Corp brought projects, people, and progress into one shared workspace. Everyone could see what mattered, who was doing what, and where things stood—without another status meeting.
