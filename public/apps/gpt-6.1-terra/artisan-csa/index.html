Get in touch ↗
What exactly is a CSA share?+

CSA stands for community-supported agriculture. You sign up for a share of our harvest and receive a changing selection of produce as the seasons unfold.

What comes in my box?+

Each box is filled with a variety of what’s freshest on the farm, from spring greens to autumn squash. The exact mix changes with the weather and the season.

Can I skip a week or change my schedule?+

Yes! Get in touch before your next packing day and we’ll help you adjust your schedule or arrange for a friend to collect your share.

How does pickup and delivery work?+

Choose a neighborhood pickup spot or ask about local delivery when you join. We’ll share the details and pickup times before your first box.

Are your add-ons suitable for everyone?+

Eggs contain egg and our sourdough contains wheat. Please contact us about ingredients or other allergies before adding an item to your share.
