What we do

Four programs. One measurable theory of change.

Each program sets public targets and reports against them — hit or miss. Here's where we landed this year.

Water & Sanitation

Community-owned wells, rainwater capture, and hygiene training with local maintenance cooperatives.

104,000people with year-round clean water

Education First

Scholarships, teacher stipends, and classroom rebuilding to keep schools open and affordable.

96%grade-6 completion rate among scholars

Food Security

Seed banks, drought-resistant crops, and farmer co-ops that turn subsistence plots into surplus.

3,100farming households above the hunger line

Community Health

Mobile clinics, maternal care, and trained village health workers reaching the last mile.

−38%drop in child mortality in served villages

The long arc

Twelve years, one milestone at a time

2013

Founded in a borrowed classroom

Three volunteers, one well drill, and a promise: publish every number, every year.

2016

First independent audit

Program-spend ratio verified at 89¢ on the dollar. We set a public target: 94¢.

2019

Education First launches

1,000 scholarships in year one. Grade-6 completion among scholars hits 88% — 21 points above district average.

2022

100,000th life reached

Cumulative reach crosses six figures as the farmer co-op model spreads to 14 communities.

2025

The 94¢ target — met

Record year: 128,450 lives reached, 94.2¢ per program dollar, and our lowest overhead ever.
