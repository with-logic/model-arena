About this

A single-file tribute to openai.fm — the place where you could hear OpenAI's text-to-speech voices before you ever needed an API key. Pick a persona, type anything, press play.

Audio here is rendered with your device's built-in speech synthesis, tuned per voice with its own pitch and cadence, so every browser gets a slightly different studio. The waveform reacts in real time as words are spoken.
