questions from the porch

FAQ & say hello

Whatever peaked that week. A typical box is 6–13 items depending on size — mostly vegetables, sometimes fruit, herbs, or nursery starts in May. Each share ships with a handwritten card of what's in it and how we'd cook it (the kohlrabi slaw has its own fan club).

We pack on Wednesday and drive Thursday. Inside the 12-mile radius, a share appears on your porch by 7 pm — cooler bags come back next week. Outside the ring, choose one of four pickup stops or the honor-box farm stand.

Mark it on the swap list on your card and leave it out — our driver swaps it for something else from the week's harvest, no charge. There's also a standing "no" list at checkout (looking at you, radishes) and a "surprise me" toggle for the brave.

No synthetic pesticides or fertilizers — we're Certified Naturally Grown and all ten partner farms follow the same standard. We farm with compost, cover crops, ladybugs, and a frankly unreasonable number of row covers.

Yes — up to 4 free pauses per season, flip the switch in your member page by Monday night. Paused weeks can be donated to the Valley Food Pantry in your name, which is the nicest vacation souvenir going.

Pay the season in full (saves 5%), or split into 3 installments at no interest. EBT/SNAP matches double your produce dollars through our Valley Food Fund — a $20 share becomes $40 of vegetables.
