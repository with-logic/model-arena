Problem → Solution

Growth broke the spreadsheet era.

Nimbus tripled its shipment volume in two years. The billing stack that worked at 700K shipments a year buckled at 2 million — and the cracks showed up as cash-flow risk.

The problem

Eleven spreadsheets, one fragile truth

Every shipment generated accessorial fees — detention, liftgate, fuel surcharges — that lived in email threads and hand-keyed tabs. Finance reconciled at month-end, when it was already too late.

  • 30 hours a week on manual entryDispatchers re-keyed rate data into invoices; errors cascaded downstream.
  • $1.9M in unbilled accessorialsFees were earned but never invoiced — invisible until an audit surfaced them.
  • 19-day average invoice cycleDisputes took weeks to resolve because no one could prove what was agreed.
  • Zero margin visibilityLeadership couldn't price lanes confidently — lane-level P&L took a month to assemble.
“We were growing 40% a year and bleeding cash at the same time. That should not be possible.”
The solution

One system of record for every load

Nimbus consolidated quoting, dispatch, billing, and reconciliation into Flowbase. Rate tables, accessorial rules, and customer contracts became structured data — enforced at the point of entry, not at month-end.

  • Auto-generated invoicesEvery completed load produces a line-item invoice from contracted rates — zero re-keying.
  • Accessorial capture at the sourceDrivers log detention and accessorial events in-app; fees flow straight to billing.
  • Dispute resolution in hoursA complete audit trail per load — POD, rates, comms — collapses disputes on sight.
  • Live lane-level P&LLeadership sees margin per lane, per customer, per day — and repriced 200+ lanes in quarter one.
“Day one in Flowbase, we found revenue we didn't know we had.”
How the solution works

Three moves that changed the economics.

Structured intake

Quotes, contracts, and rate tables become machine-readable the moment a load books. No free-text, no ghost spreadsheets, no ambiguity at invoice time.

92% of manual entry eliminated

Automated billing engine

Invoices generate on delivery confirmation, priced against contracted rates with every accessorial attached. Exceptions route to a human; the other 96% just go out.

Invoice cycle: 19 days → 3

Margin intelligence

Every dollar of revenue and cost lands on the lane it belongs to. Nimbus now kills or reprices underperforming lanes weekly instead of quarterly.

+310% gross margin per lane
