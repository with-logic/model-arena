The program

A six-month curriculum engineered like a product roadmap

Every phase has deliverables, measurements, and a graduation standard. You will see your own numbers improve.

WEEKS 0–2

Foundation & Install Setup week

Our team installs your machine and grinder, tests your water, and calibrates to it. Baseline cupping session maps your sensory vocabulary. You pull your first coached shot and we record everything — this is "before" you'll celebrate later.

MONTH 1

Dial-In Mastery

Grind, dose, yield, and temperature under deliberate practice protocols. Graduation standard: consistently hit a 20–21% extraction window on three unfamiliar coffees, blind, within five minutes of first crack of information.

MONTHS 2–3

The Roaster's Chair

Charge temp, rate of rise, first crack, development time ratio. You'll roast 40+ batches against reference profiles, refractometer-verify every one, and build three original profiles your coach green-lights for competition format.

MONTHS 4–5

Signature Espresso

Blend design, milk science for cortado-class drinks, and pressure-profiling on your Slayer. You develop your signature drink and defend it — sensorily, with data — at a live member cupping.

MONTH 6

Graduation Cupping Finals

A blind, scored evaluation against our graduate standard — judged by two coaches plus an independent Q grader. Pass, and you keep your roaster at no extra cost for a year, join the alumni Pull-Off circuit, and get first-refusal on rare lots.
