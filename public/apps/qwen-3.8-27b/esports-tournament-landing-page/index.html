Code of Conduct

Applies to all teams, staff, and attendees. Violations may result in disqualification or ejection.

1. Fair Play. No cheating, exploiting, or interference. Competitive integrity is non-negotiable.

2. Respect. Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination toward players, staff, or fans is prohibited.

3. Sportsmanship. Accept decisions of referees and officials. Disputes go through the appeals process, not the crowd.

4. Substance Policy. Zero tolerance for performance-enhancing or impairing substances.

5. Venue Rules. Follow all arena, safety, and local regulations. Photography per the media policy.

6. Consequences. Warnings, fines, match forfeits, or full disqualification at the organizers' discretion.
