Why Aura One

Engineered to disappear.

Four decisions we made so you never have to think about your watch again.

01

Brightness, unleashed

A 3,000-nit LTPO display punches through desert sun and night shifts alike. 120 Hz when you need it, 1 Hz when you don't — always on, always legible.

Two weeks. One charge.

The Aura A1 chip sips power so hard that a full week of use is barely a dent. Top up from 0–80% in 25 minutes on the magnetic charger.
