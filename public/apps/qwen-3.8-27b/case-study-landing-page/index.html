01 · The Challenge

Six tools. Six versions of the truth. Zero confidence.

By 2025, XYZ Corp's revenue data lived in six different places — and every number required a manual reconciliation before anyone trusted it. Growth had outpaced the spreadsheet era, and it was showing.

01

Six tools, six versions of the truth

Revenue data was scattered across the CRM, billing, spreadsheets, support, and the warehouse. Every report started with a week of manual reconciliation.

02

40 hours a week, lost to reporting

RevOps hand-built the same decks every single week. Leadership made decisions on data that was already two weeks old.

03

At-risk accounts surfaced too late

Sales and support never shared a view of customer health. Renewal risk was discovered in QBRs — not weeks before it became churn.

04

Forecasting was a guess

Quarterly forecasts missed by roughly 40%. Board reporting took two weeks and at least one weekend.
