Before you join

The things neighbors ask most. Anything else — write to us.

A share is a standing order for a packed vegetable box — 18–22 seasonal items, plus whatever add-ons you choose. You’re paying for the season’s labor up front, which is how we can keep the price flat and the produce honest.

Anytime. Log in and pause up to two weeks per season — no forms, no guilt. Going on vacation? Your box can sit with a neighbor or donate to our food-share shelf at the farm stand.

Swap it at pickup for an equivalent item, or tell us and we’ll make it right — no photos required. If an item arrives bad, it’s on us, full stop.

Anywhere inside the 10-mile radius of the farm, Tuesday through Sunday, plus five partner pickup spots a little farther out. Outside the circle? Join the waitlist below — we add one new route each year.

Mid-March through late December. Winter shares are smaller and heartier — think cabbages, squash, and garlic — and you can pause in January if the cold gets to you before the garden does.

Yes. Split the season into four payments, or pay monthly. No interest, no fees — just a standing order we’re glad to have.
