The problem

You've bought everything.
It still tastes like a gas station.

Gear is the easy part. The hard part is feedback: someone who can taste what your variables are actually doing, and a curriculum that turns random tweaks into a repeatable shot.

01 / FEEDBACK

Gear without feedback

A $2,400 grinder is a very expensive way to make the same sour shot twice. Without someone tasting with you, "dialing in" is just guessing with good equipment.

02 / TRANSFER

Recipes that don't transfer

Your barista's ratios assume their machine, their water, their dose, their beans. Yours is none of those. Copying a recipe copies the problem, not the shot.

03 / CALIBRATION

No one to taste with you

Palate is calibrated socially. Solo coffee nerds plateau around "pretty good" and stay there for years, because nobody is telling them what the last 10% is.
