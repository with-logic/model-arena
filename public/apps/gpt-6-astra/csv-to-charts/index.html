No formulas. No complicated setup. Just a clearer picture.

Bring your data

Upload or drop a CSV or TSV, up to 5 MB and 5,000 rows. Include column names in the first row and at least one numeric column.

2
Find the right view

Choose from six chart types, map your columns, and pick a palette. Click the small series color to customize it. Pie and donut charts use one value series.

3
Make it worth sharing

Give your chart a title, then export a crisp PNG or scalable SVG. Hover or keyboard-focus chart marks to explore their values.

Everything happens in your browser. Your workspace is saved on this device when browser storage is available.
