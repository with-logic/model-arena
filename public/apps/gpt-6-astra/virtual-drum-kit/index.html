Find your groove.

Your own little studio, right here. Grab headphones, turn it up, and make something.

01   Play the kit

Click a drum or use its keyboard key. On your phone, try Pad view for more room to tap. Start with a kick on Space, a snare on A, and a steady hi-hat on W.

02   Keep your time

Set your tempo and turn on the metronome. The four lights count the beat. The click stays out of your recording.

03   Make it a take

Hit Record, play, then Stop. Listen back, loop your groove, or download it as a WAV. Each new recording replaces the last; your latest take is saved in this browser.
