Make time for one thing.

The Pomodoro method makes big tasks feel smaller, with a little room to recharge.

  1. 1
    Choose your one thing.Name your task, set your rhythm, and start a focus session.
  2. 2
    Give it your attention.Focus until the beep. Pause whenever life needs you.
  3. 3
    Take a real break.Step away, stretch, or get some water. After four focus sessions, take a longer break.
