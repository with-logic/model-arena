How we measure our impact

People first. Numbers with context.

Our reporting period is January 1–December 31, 2025. People reached includes 4,800 education participants, 6,400 people with improved water access, and 1,250 livelihood participants. These distinct program groups total 12,450 people across 18 communities. Growth is measured against 9,730 people in 2024 and rounded to the nearest whole percent.

What our measures mean

School retention is the share of supported students still enrolled at year end. Water access means a functioning community water point within a 30-minute round trip. Livelihood income change compares participant-reported household earnings at entry and after 12 months. Financial percentages reflect total annual spending, not income. This concept report uses illustrative data and stories; it is not an audited statement.
