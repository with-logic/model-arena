A few things to know

Who can compete?

Five-player teams with a designated captain can apply. All players must be at least 16. Invitational seeds and open qualifier teams make up the final 16.

What does my ticket include?

Day passes include all matches on your chosen day. Weekend passes cover all three days. VIP adds priority entry, premium seating, and an exclusive event pack.

Can I watch online for free?

Yes. The main broadcast is free, with English commentary. Coverage begins September 18 at 12:00 CEST. Until then, enjoy our arena preview above.

Is there an age restriction?

Attendees must be 16 or older. Guests under 18 must attend with a parent or legal guardian. Bring a valid photo ID for entry.
