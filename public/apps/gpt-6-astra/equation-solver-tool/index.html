    A note on notation.

    Write your equation naturally, then press Enter. Equate solves linear and quadratic equations with one variable and explains how to get there.

    To writeUse thisExample
    A power ^ or ²x^2 + 3x = 4
    Multiplication* or adjacent terms3(x + 2) = 12
    A fraction/x/3 + 2 = 5
    A square rootsqrt(number) or √x + sqrt(9) = 7
    A different variablex, y, z, or t2y + 1 = 9

    What can I solve?

    Use one variable per equation. Parentheses, decimals, constant fractions, and square roots of nonnegative numbers are supported. Variables in denominators, variable square roots, trigonometric functions, and equations of degree greater than two are not supported.
