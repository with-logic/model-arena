The overview

Growing fast. Staying connected.

Great ideas weren’t in short supply at XYZ Corp. With 250 people building technology for a growing customer base, momentum was everywhere. But the way work happened hadn’t kept up.

Teams were switching between spreadsheets, chasing updates in chat, and piecing together the bigger picture. They needed one place to turn all that energy into progress. That’s where Align came in.

The challenge

More tools. Less clarity.

As XYZ Corp grew, so did the gaps between teams. Every department had its own way of working—and getting a simple status update often meant another meeting.

  • Project updates scattered across five different tools
  • Manual handoffs that slowed down good work
  • No shared view of priorities, ownership, or progress
The solution

One place. Everyone in sync.

XYZ Corp brought their projects and workflows into Align. With shared workspaces, automated handoffs, and a clear view of what’s next, the team could finally focus on moving work forward.
