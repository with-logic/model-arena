Ask us anything

CSA stands for Community Supported Agriculture. You become part of a farm’s season by subscribing to a share of its harvest. Your support helps farmers plan ahead, and you receive a regular box of fresh, local produce. We share the abundance—and the occasional surprises—of growing food.

The harvest sets the menu, so your produce share is a seasonal selection of 7–9 varieties. We send a preview before each box and include recipes to help you enjoy everything. You can make it your own with optional eggs, flowers, and sourdough.

Life happens. Skip a box or pause your share by letting us know at least 72 hours before your delivery. You won’t be charged for skipped boxes. You can also gift your harvest to a neighbor or ask us to donate it to a local community pantry.

Home delivery is available in our Portland delivery area for $5 per box. Check your ZIP above to see if we reach your neighborhood. Pickup is free at any of our three locations. We’ll confirm your delivery day or pickup details before your first harvest.

Our produce shares are plant based and have no wheat ingredients. Optional eggs contain egg, and sourdough contains wheat. Add-ons are packed separately, but we can’t guarantee an allergen-free packing environment. Please contact us about specific allergies before joining so we can talk through your needs.
