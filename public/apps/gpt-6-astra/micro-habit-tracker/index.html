Your streak counts consecutive completed days. An unfinished today won’t break it. A missed day ends it.

Rest is part of the routine.

Use a habit’s ••• menu to skip today, or “Skip today” to rest all unfinished habits. Skips preserve streaks without adding to them, and don’t count toward completion rates. Right-click a past checkmark for day options.
